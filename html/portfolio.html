Ceramic is a composition of research practices, innovation, and materiality, forming a conceptually cohesive collection and runway exhibition.

This collection exists as an ongoing body of work and research focused on developing textiles through the employment of alternative materials such as paper yarn, synthetic paper, polymer, and natural resin.

The designer's practice is concerned with subverting the traditional value systems of fashion and perceived material worth, questioning the modern women's wardrobe while offering an identity of androgyny with an aesthetic informed by masculine uniform.
