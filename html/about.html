Wilson Ong

Wilson Ong is a New Zealand-based fashion designer working across garment construction, costume design, and collaborative practice. His work is grounded in considered materiality — precise construction, natural fabrics, and a quiet attention to how clothing is worn and held.

He has shown collections through his graduate and undergraduate years, and continues to develop an independent label producing garments made in New Zealand. Alongside this, he has designed costumes for theatre productions including Pork & Poll Taxes (2021) and This Room Is An Island (2023), and has collaborated on projects including the National Contemporary Art Awards (2019).

Available for commissions and collaborations.
